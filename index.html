How to use Trip Pins

1. Join the trip

  1. Got an invite link by WhatsApp or text? Just open it. You're connected; there's nothing to type.
  2. No link? Tap Connect, paste the URL and the Key from the person who set up the trip, and tap Connect.

2. Put it on your home screen

  1. In Chrome, tap the menu ⋮ (top right).
  2. Tap Add to Home screen or Install.

Now it opens like a normal app, and it remembers the trip.

  1. Open this page in Safari (other browsers can't do this step).
  2. Tap the Share button (the square with an arrow), then Add to Home Screen → Add.
  3. Open Trip Pins from your home screen. It starts blank: that's normal on iPhone.
  4. Go back to WhatsApp or Messages, press and hold the invite link, and tap Copy.
  5. In Trip Pins, tap Connect → Paste → Connect. You only do this once.

3. Save places from Google Maps

  1. In the Google Maps app, open a place you like.
  2. Tap Share, then pick Trip Pins.
  3. Type a quick note on why you want to go, and tap Save.

Trip Pins not in the share menu? Open it once from your home screen, then try again. It may also be hiding under More.

  1. In the Google Maps app, open a place you like.
  2. Tap Share → Copy link.
  3. Open Trip Pins, tap + Add, then Paste. It looks the place up for you.
  4. Type a quick note on why you want to go, and tap Save.

iPhones don't let web apps sit in the share menu. If you save lots of places, ask whoever set up the trip for the "Save to Trip Pins" Shortcut; it saves straight from Google Maps' share menu.

No link handy? Tap + Add and type a name, like “Kuromon Market”.

4. Using the shared list

  • Go opens Google Maps with directions to that spot. 🚆 opens public transport directions (trains, subway, buses) from where you are.
  • List: tap 🧭 Nearest first (and allow location) when you're out on the trip. Map shows everyone's places as coloured pins, and you as a blue dot.
  • Visited places are hidden in the List and faded on the Map. ✓ Show visited brings them back.
  • ✓ marks a place as visited. The pencil fixes a name, a note or a typo.
  • It's one live list. Anything you add, edit or tick shows on everyone else's phone too, within about a minute and a half.

5. Plan the days

  • Set the trip's first and last day in ⚙ → This trip. Everyone on the trip shares them.
  • Tap 📅 Plan on a place to put it on a day. The Plan tab shows every day; the arrows change the order.
  • The route button sorts a day into a short walking order, starting from its first place. The pin button shows that day on the map, numbered.
  • The 📅 All days chip above the List and Map picks one day to show, or the places not planned yet.
  • Days that have passed fold into Past days: ✓ for places you went, ! for ones you missed. Move a missed place with 📅, or take it off the plan with ×.
  • My Today is just for you. On a trip day it starts with that day's plan; tap the star on any place to add or remove it.
  • The search box finds places by name or by what's in Why go?.

Stuck?

  • Places look out of date? Tap the refresh arrow at the top.
  • “Key changed” message? Ask the organiser for a fresh invite link and open it.
  • Want to bring someone else in? ⚙ → Add another phone sends them a link.
